We Believe Skincare Should Come from the Soil, Not a Synthetic Laboratory.

Every drop in our collection is extracted by hydraulic pressure alone, without chemical hexane solvents, steam stripping, or synthetic stabilization. By keeping temperatures beneath 27°C, we preserve the uncorrupted vitality of the living plant.

Every bottle bears a unique batch seal and QR code linking directly to third-party Gas Chromatography–Mass Spectrometry (GC-MS) purity test reports. You see the exact molecular profile of your harvest down to the fractional percentage.
